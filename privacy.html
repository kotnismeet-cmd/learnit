Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

LearnIt turns a topic or your own study material into lessons, quizzes and a tutor chat. This page explains what information the site handles, why, and what choices you have.

What we collect

  • An anonymous visitor ID. When you first use the site, your browser receives a random ID stored in a cookie called lid. It keeps your courses and progress separate from other visitors. It does not contain your name or email, and there are no accounts or passwords.
  • What you give the site. Topics, goals and levels you enter; text from files you upload; web addresses you paste and the text of those pages; your answers to lesson and quiz questions; and the messages you send to the tutor.
  • Progress data. Your courses, lesson results, per-concept scores, XP and streak.
  • Usage counts. To enforce daily limits, we count how many times your visitor ID and your network (IP) address have used each feature that day. These counts older than about a week are deleted automatically.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and requested page) for security and operation.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To build your course, lessons, quizzes and tutor answers from your material.
  • To remember your progress and show it back to you.
  • To prevent abuse and keep costs under control, through the daily limits.

We do not sell your information.

Who else receives it

  • Anthropic. Your topic, uploaded text, pasted page text, quiz results summary and chat messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate lessons, quizzes and answers. When web research is switched on, Claude may also search the web for your topic. Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
  • Wikipedia. If web research is on, your topic is sent to Wikipedia's public search to find articles.
  • Websites you link. If you paste a URL, our server downloads that page.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the database and runs the site.

Do not upload or type anything you would not want processed by these services, such as passwords, government ID numbers, health records or other people's private information.

Cookies and local storage

The only cookie is lid, described above. It is needed for the site to work, is not used for tracking, and lasts up to two years. Clearing your cookies gives you a new, empty visitor ID. Your old data then becomes unreachable to you but may remain on the server until it is removed.

How long we keep it

  • Courses and their sources, lessons and scores are kept until you delete the course.
  • XP and streak are kept with your visitor ID.
  • Usage counts are removed after about a week.

Your choices

  • Delete a course. Use the Delete button on the home page. This removes that course's sources, text chunks, lessons and scores.
  • Ask us. To have everything tied to your visitor ID deleted, or to ask any privacy question, contact us at the address below. We may need the visitor ID value from your lid cookie to find your data.
  • Turn off research. Untick "Also research the internet" when creating a course to avoid web search and Wikipedia lookups.

Security

The Anthropic API key stays on the server and is never sent to your browser. Traffic is encrypted when the site is served over HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive information out of your material.

Children

LearnIt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.
